THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Consistency starts
with the behavior.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I pay attention to the details that connect one screen to the next: what changes, what stays the same, and what someone can do when the expected result does not arrive. My technical communication background helps me explain those decisions as well as design them.

At FactorLab, I develop SmartTagIt flows and reusable insight widgets, then walk developers through states and alternatives in Figma. The examples here show that product-level work. They include open interpretation questions and identify what the available release evidence supports.

For Coinbase, I would begin with a recurring pattern, review how it behaves in the products that use it, and prototype the states with design and engineering. I would bring the same care to an AI-assisted revision: check whether it preserves the requirements before treating a cleaner result as a better one.
