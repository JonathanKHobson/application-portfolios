THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Make the behavior clear.
Then make the words fit.

A good question can change
the whole story.

At FactorLab, I design SmartTagIt workflows and document their behavior for engineering. The useful questions are often specific: what has been saved, what remains after a connection drops, and which action lets someone continue?

My research and education work brings a similar concern. At Oak, I connected findings to navigation and login-feedback alternatives. In a recent Oppia review, I asked engineering to clarify autosave and save-on-exit before finalizing the dialog guidance. At ASU, I developed learning materials and proposed conversational workflows with human review.

Nava Labs’ caseworker tools connect answers to sources and keep staff reviewing the result. For a future project, I would learn from people using and delivering the service, map where the rules or system states become confusing, and work with the team to test a manageable change. This is a proposed approach. My portfolio shows operational and education work; I have not presented it as prior government-contract delivery.
