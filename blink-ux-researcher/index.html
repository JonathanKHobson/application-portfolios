THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

What does the client
need to decide?

A good question can change
the whole story.

My work begins with that question. A team might ask for a survey when it needs to observe a task, or request a new feature when the barrier is choosing what to do first. I help make the uncertainty specific enough to investigate and discuss together.

At Oak, I combined structured and open-response survey material, coded qualitative observations in Dovetail, and evaluated interface usability. Today, I connect research questions with prototypes and engineering decisions at FactorLab, and serve as UX Research Lead in my volunteer work with Oppia. My background in documentary storytelling and technical communication helps me make a complicated finding understandable.

For a Blink engagement, I would want to agree early on the decision, audience, constraints, and review points. I value enough autonomy to do the work carefully and enough feedback to make sure it stays useful to the team. I am available for freelance projects and embedded research roles.
