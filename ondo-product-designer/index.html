THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Understand the state.
Then design the action.

A good question can change
the whole story.

At FactorLab, I design SmartTagIt web and mobile workflows and document their behavior for engineering. The useful questions are often specific: is this information saved, what remains after a connection drops, and which action lets someone continue?

My research work helps me make those decisions explicit. At Oak, I connected usability findings to navigation alternatives, login feedback and shared color guidance. At KeepMake, I helped distinguish a usability study from an activation experiment and proposed a smaller learning plan the team could manage.

For Ondo, I would begin by learning the product rules with Product, Engineering and Research, then map one client journey through its ordinary, waiting and exception states. I would use prototypes to check comprehension and agree on success measures before calling a change an improvement. This is a proposed approach.
