THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Give the team
a useful next step.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I start with the decision a team needs to make. A survey can reveal reported barriers; observation can show the concerns already present in a community. A usability evaluation can make a particular interaction problem concrete. I choose the method around what the evidence needs to support.

At Oak AI, I led survey work, coded qualitative material, and connected usability findings to product recommendations. At FactorLab, I work with designers and engineers to carry findings into Figma flows and annotated handoffs. My technical communication background helps me explain the reasoning across those conversations.

Blenderbox’s work on public services is a setting where that care matters. My experience in university learning environments and current volunteer work with Oppia have kept accessibility and understandable interactions close to the work. I would bring those habits to the questions and constraints of a client project.
