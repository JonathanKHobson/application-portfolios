THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

An AI workflow needs
an understandable review process.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I built and released Prompt Compass, a local tool for diagnosing, improving, comparing and reusing prompts. One designed regression case checks whether a shorter rewrite retains required output fields and a human review condition. It makes a technical change visible enough to discuss.

My ASU instructional design and Coursera teaching work give that review process a practical learning structure. People need a representative task, a useful demonstration and a chance to inspect and revise their own attempt. At FactorLab, I also document what the assistant does when an answer is interrupted or a connection is lost.

For an initial Nimble Gravity engagement, I would map one client process with its business owner, identify approved sources and review responsibilities, and build a demonstration around representative inputs. I would agree on baseline task success, rework and repeated use, then use workshop feedback and observed performance to improve the guidance. This is a proposed approach.
