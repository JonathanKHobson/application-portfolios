THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Make the next step
easy to understand.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I am interested in the moment when someone has enough information to choose a next step. The design needs to help them understand what they are seeing, what a choice means, and how to recover if the expected result does not arrive.

At FactorLab, I developed insight widgets and mobile interaction states, then worked through alternatives with developers. At Oak AI, I studied the gap between a creator audience’s interest and its ability to act. The examples here pair the artifact with the reasoning and the limits of the evidence.

For a ShopMy feature, I would work with the team to understand one creator or brand task and what is already known about it. I would prototype the decisions across web and mobile, review the tradeoffs, and test comprehension and recovery. That is a proposed collaboration approach; the work shown here comes from other products.
