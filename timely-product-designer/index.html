THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Understand the constraint.
Make the choice clear.

A good question can change
the whole story.

At Oak, I connected a usability evaluation to navigation and login-feedback alternatives with the lead UX designer. A useful recommendation needs enough detail for the team to discuss it and decide what to change.

At FactorLab, I design SmartTagIt workflows and document their behavior for engineering. The questions are specific: what has been saved, what remains after a connection drops, and which action lets someone continue? Reusable patterns help those answers stay consistent.

My ASU work included digital learning materials and conversational AI flows with a proposed human-review step. Timely’s scenario comparisons make scheduling trade-offs visible. School master scheduling would be a new domain. At Timely, I would start by observing one difficult scheduling decision, map its constraints with the team and test a focused prototype. This is a proposed starting approach, not a delivered school-scheduling project.
