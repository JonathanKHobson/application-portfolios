THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

What helps someone
take the next step?

A good question can change
the whole story.

My background runs through electronic media, technical communication, learning design, and UX research. Each asks me to pay attention to the person doing the work: what they understand, where they hesitate, and what they need to try next.

At Coursera, I created a guided project for people learning to use ChatGPT. At ASU, I developed learning materials and proposed learner-support workflows with mentor review. I now serve as UX Research Lead in my volunteer work with Oppia, where questions about a lesson’s exit behavior become questions about what a learner can expect to happen to their progress.

For Wikimedia, I would want to understand a contributor’s real task before choosing a learning format. A course, a short demonstration, or a change to the surrounding workflow should follow from that evidence. I would make the first version small enough to try with contributors and revise from what they can do independently.
