My work sits between research, interaction design, and the practical constraints of building software. At FactorLab, I develop SmartTagIt page directions, workflow alternatives, and engineering guidance. About Me is a useful example: a comparison can look clear while leaving its meaning unresolved. I explored both the presentation and the questions behind it.

At Oak AI, I connected evaluation findings to navigation alternatives and specific feedback changes, working with the lead UX designer on the recommendations. At ASU and Oppia, I have worked on learning experiences where the person’s context and next action shape the design problem.

Prompt Compass adds an independent product example. I built and released its public beta, then continued to develop local evaluation examples. For GoGuardian, I would bring that habit of making a requirement or product judgment concrete enough to inspect, discuss, and improve.
