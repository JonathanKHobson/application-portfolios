THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Make the evidence
useful to the team.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I start with the decision the team needs to make. A survey can reveal reported barriers, while observing a task can show where a flow breaks down. The method matters because it determines what a finding can support and what still needs another check.

At Oak AI, I led audience survey work and connected usability findings to product recommendations. At FactorLab, I translate findings into Figma flows and annotated engineering handoffs. My technical communication background helps me make the reasoning understandable across research, design, and development.

I also build with AI. Prompt Compass helps make prompt requirements and changes available for inspection. For Apollo, I would bring that habit to research tools: use them to make evidence easier to work with, preserve the source context, and keep the analyst’s interpretation open to review.
