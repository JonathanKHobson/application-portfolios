THE THREAD THROUGH MY WORK

Start with what
people need to know.

A good question can change
the whole story.

I look for the question behind a complicated screen. What is the person trying to understand? What information matters first? What can they do next? My technical communication background helps me organize that content and explain the design choices to the team.

At FactorLab, I developed reusable insight widgets and explored their information order, density, and interpretation. Flint added another challenge: connecting a question to waiting, an answer, and recovery when something interrupts the flow. At Oak AI, usability findings became navigation and feedback recommendations.

For a Fueled content project, I would begin with the audience’s task and the team’s existing content models and publishing constraints. I would use representative content to explore hierarchy and reusable patterns, then review the behavior with users, editors, and engineering. This is a proposed approach grounded in the interface work shown here.
