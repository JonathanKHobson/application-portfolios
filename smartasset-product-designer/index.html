My background in technical communication and learning design shapes how I approach product work. I pay attention to what a person needs to understand before acting, and where the interface leaves them to guess.

At FactorLab, I design web and mobile interactions and explain them through Figma prototypes, annotated handoffs, and discussion with developers. At Oak AI, I connected research and usability evaluation to navigation and error-feedback recommendations. I currently volunteer with Oppia as UX Research Lead, reviewing how lesson behavior matches what learners can expect.

For SmartAsset, I would work within the existing design system, use the team’s research and experiment results to define a focused change, and review the next attempt. I want the prototype and its rationale to be clear enough for a product pod to challenge and improve.
