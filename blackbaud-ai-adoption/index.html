At ASU Knowledge Enterprise, I developed digital learning materials and workshops with accessibility improvements and AI-supported content workflows. My Coursera project turns a familiar content task into a sequence beginners can follow, inspect and revise.

At FactorLab, I define interaction states and annotate behavior for engineering. An interrupted answer and a lost connection need different recovery guidance. That attention to a person’s next action also matters when a new AI workflow changes daily work.

For an initial adoption effort, I would learn one team’s task and readiness barriers, agree on a worked example and reference aid, and test the guidance with representative employees. I would define the baseline and success measures with the product owner, then use observed difficulties and repeat-task evidence to revise the materials. This is a proposed approach, not a report of an enterprise rollout.
